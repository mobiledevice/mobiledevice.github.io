A screen resolution detector is a tool that reads your device's display properties — including screen width and height in pixels, viewport size, device pixel ratio (DPR), and orientation — and shows them to you instantly in your browser.

Open this page and the tool automatically detects your screen resolution, viewport size, pixel density, and orientation. No button press needed — the data appears as soon as the page loads.

Device pixel ratio (DPR) is the ratio between physical pixels and CSS pixels. A DPR of 2 means the screen has twice as many physical pixels as CSS pixels, which is common on modern smartphones and Retina displays.

Yes. The screen resolution detector works on all modern devices: iPhone, Android phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.

The screen resolution is the total number of physical pixels on your display. The viewport is the visible area inside the browser window, excluding browser UI like toolbars, scrollbars, and the address bar.

Orientation indicates whether your screen is in landscape (wider than tall) or portrait (taller than wide) mode. Rotating your device switches between the two.
